Allama Muhammad Iqbal - My Favourite Poet

Introduction

Allama Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938) is my favourite poet and philosopher. He is the national poet of Pakistan and is known as the Poet of the East (Shair-e-Mashriq). He wrote poetry in Urdu and Persian that inspired Muslims of the subcontinent. His work focuses on selfhood (Khudi), spirituality, and the revival of the Muslim Ummah. As a student of English Literature, I admire his philosophical depth and his powerful message for youth.

Famous Works

Some of his most famous books and collections of poetry are:

  • Bang-e-Dara (The Call of the Marching Bell) - 1924
  • Bal-e-Jibril (Gabriel's Wing) - 1935
  • Zarb-e-Kaleem (The Stroke of Moses) - 1936
  • Asrar-e-Khudi (Secrets of the Self) - 1915
